# Implementation of a Hierarchical SDN Control Workflow for Autonomous Capacity Scaling in Multi-Layer Networks


**Authors:** _**L. Vettori**_, Ricardo Martínez, Ramon Casellas, F. Javier Vílchez, Josep Maria Fàbrega,  and Raul Muñoz.  
**Conference:** 2026 26th International Conference on Transparent Optical Networks (ICTON), Prague, Czech Republic, 2026  
**DOI:** <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/ICTON71309.2026.11705334" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">10.1109/ICTON71309.2026.11705334</a>

## Abstract

Cross-domain closed-loop control across heterogeneous access, packet, and optical network segments remains an open challenge in multi-layer SDN orchestration. In addition, correlating heterogeneous domain metrics into coordinated control decisions is still challenging in practice. This paper presents the implementation of a hierarchical SDN control framework that collects multi-domain telemetry, correlates congestion conditions across access, packet, and optical segments, and executes an autonomous workflow to upgrade and downscale optical capacity according to service and load conditions.
