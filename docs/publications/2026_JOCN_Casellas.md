# Unified Control of MultiBand over SDM Optical Networks based on OpenROADM device models

**Authors:** R. Casellas, R.Morro, L. Nadal, F. J. Vílchez, V. Lohani, _**L. Vettori**_, R. Martínez, R. Vilalta, and R. Muñoz.    
**Journal:** Journal of Optical Communications and Networking (JOCN), July 2026.  
**DOI:** <a href="https://doi.org/10.1364/JOCN.605854" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">10.1364/JOCN.605854</a>

## Abstract

This paper presents a proof-of-concept implementation of a software-defined control framework for multiband over spatial-division multiplexing (MBoSDM) optical networks based on OpenROADM device data models. As optical networks evolve to meet rapidly increasing capacity and flexibility demands, the joint exploitation of spectral and spatial resources emerges as a key enabler for scalable multi-dimensional networking. However, most existing standards primarily target C-band operation and do not fully address multiband and SDM capabilities. In this work, we apply OpenROADM device models and develop SDN agents for multiband sliceable bandwidth-variable transceivers (S-BVTs), ROADMs, and hybrid multigranular nodes supporting wavelength, band, and spatial switching. A disaggregated optical network architecture is implemented, together with an enhanced SDN control plane based on our FlexOpt controller using NETCONF/YANG interfaces and NMDA extensions. The proposed solution supports multi-layer provisioning across OTS/SDM and DWDM layers, including path computation and resource allocation strategies tailored to MBoSDM environments. Experimental validation demonstrates coordinated service provisioning over multiband (C, L, S) and SDM domains, confirming the feasibility of OpenROADM-based control for next-generation optical networks. The results highlight the potential of model-driven, open, and programmable control frameworks to enable efficient, scalable, and interoperable MBoSDM network deployments.
