# Low-level Integration with SDN Control Plane, and Experimental Validation of the Programmable MBoSDM Node Prototype


**Authors:** F. J. Vílchez, _**L. Vettori**_, R. Casellas, L. Nadal, M. S. Moreolo and R. Muñoz.   
**Conference:** 2026 26th International Conference on Transparent Optical Networks (ICTON), Prague, Czech Republic, 2026  
**DOI:** <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/ICTON71309.2026.11705024" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">10.1109/ICTON71309.2026.11705024</a>

## Abstract

Building on the recent developments of multi-band over spatial division multiplexing (MBoSDM) switching prototype and SDN controller, in this paper we present the required low-level upgrades and definitions to achieve a complete integration between the data and control planes, adopting MBoSDM technology. The integrated solution is experimentally validated in a network scenario within the ADRENALINE testbed, demonstrating the dynamic provisioning of core-level and MB flex-grid cross-connections of the MBoSDM node prototype.
