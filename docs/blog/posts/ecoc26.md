Just came back from _**ECOC 2026 | 52nd European Conference on Optical Communication in Málaga**_ 🇪🇸.

Excited to have participated as a presenter in the Demo Zone.
It was a great opportunity to showcase our work, exchange ideas with experts from the optical communications community, and connect with old and new colleagues and researchers. Thank you to everyone who stopped by, asked questions, and shared their insights. It was a pleasure to be part of such an inspiring conference!
